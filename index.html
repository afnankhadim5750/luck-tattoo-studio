The studio

Luck Tattoo Studio is a custom-only shop in Tampa, FL. There’s no flash wall to pick from here — every tattoo starts as a conversation, gets drawn for one person and one body, and is worn for life.

That goes for first pieces and for cover-ups alike. Regret an old piece? Cover-ups are a speciality of the studio: we assess what’s there, design around it, and turn ink you hide into work you show off.

Black & grey, colour work, small single-session pieces or multi-session projects — the process never changes. Free consultation first, honest quote second, shortcuts never.
